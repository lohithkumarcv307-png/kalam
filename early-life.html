From Rameswaram to a dream

The story of a curious boy whose discipline, family values and love of learning helped shape a remarkable life.

Roots in Rameswaram

A childhood grounded in values

Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He grew up in a modest family where honesty, kindness, discipline and respect for others were central values.

His early years taught him resilience and the importance of learning from people around him. Curiosity became a habit, and education became a path toward a wider world.
