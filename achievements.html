CHAPTER 05 · ACHIEVEMENTS & HONOURS

A legacy measured in impact

From India’s space programme to the Presidency and a lifetime of inspiring students, explore the milestones of Dr. Kalam’s journey.

A lifetime of contribution

Milestones that shaped a nation

Search the highlights below. These achievements span science, public service, writing and the inspiration he gave to generations.

1980

Rohini satellite launch

As project director of SLV-III, Kalam led the team that successfully placed the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit.

1981

Padma Bhushan

Recognised by the Government of India for distinguished service.

1990

Padma Vibhushan

Received one of India’s highest civilian honours for contributions to science and engineering.

1997

Bharat Ratna

Awarded India’s highest civilian honour for his contribution to scientific research and public service.

1998

Pokhran-II

Played an important coordinating role in India’s nuclear tests as a scientific adviser.

2002–2007

11th President of India

Served as President and became widely known as the People’s President.

2007

King Charles II Medal

Recognised by the Royal Society, London, for contributions to science and technology.

2010

Hoover Medal

Honoured for a lifetime of professional achievements and public service.

2015

Teacher to the end

Passed away while delivering a lecture to students at IIM Shillong, reflecting his lifelong commitment to teaching.
