CHAPTER 03 · LEADERSHIP & SERVICE

The People’s President

A leader who brought the dignity of the office closer to ordinary citizens, especially young people.

Journey to Rashtrapati Bhavan

A President with a teacher’s heart

Dr. Kalam served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was widely admired for his simplicity, accessibility and commitment to education.

Even while serving in the highest constitutional office, he continued to engage with students and speak about a developed India. He believed young people should be empowered to think independently and contribute to society.

2002

2002–2007

Encouraged education, science and civic responsibility.

Teacher for life

Continued interacting with students and educators after his presidency.
