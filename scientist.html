CHAPTER 02 · SCIENCE & INNOVATION

Building a stronger India through science

A pioneering aerospace scientist whose work helped strengthen India’s space and defence capabilities.

ISRO · DRDO · National development

The scientist who looked skyward

Kalam contributed to India’s space research and guided important missile development programmes. At ISRO, he was associated with the SLV-III project, which placed the Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980.

Later, his leadership at DRDO and role in the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme earned him the popular title “Missile Man of India.” His work symbolised teamwork, persistence and technological self-reliance.
